Common Real Estate Questions & Glossary

Clear, jargon-free explanations of the most critical terms and procedures in Nigerian estate surveying, valuation, and land acquisition:

What is AGIS and why is it mandatory for any Abuja land deal?

AGIS (Abuja Geographic Information Systems) is the official computerized land registry of the Federal Capital Territory Administration (FCTA). Every legitimate plot in Abuja has an official cadastral file, registered survey coordinates, and ownership history at AGIS. An AGIS legal search verifies who truly owns the land and confirms it is free of unpaid ground rents, bank mortgages, or court injunctions.

What is the difference between a C of O and an R of O?

A Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) is the supreme government title deed in Nigeria, conferring a 99-year statutory leasehold signed by the FCT Minister. A Right of Occupancy (R of O) is the preliminary letter of offer or grant; while legally valid, it requires administrative regularization at AGIS before it becomes a full C of O. C of O plots command the highest resale values and easiest bank loan approvals.

Why use a licensed Estate Surveyor instead of a regular agent?

Informal agents earn purely on commission when a deal closes—so their commercial incentive is to push you to pay quickly regardless of title flaws. A Registered Estate Surveyor & Valuer (RSV) certified by ESVARBON and NIESV is a chartered professional carrying statutory liability and indemnity insurance. We provide unbiased, legally binding valuations and title verification that protect your money.

What does 'Open Market Value' (OMV) vs 'Forced Sale Value' (FSV) mean?

Open Market Value (OMV) is what a willing buyer would pay a willing seller under normal market marketing conditions. Forced Sale Value (FSV) is the discounted benchmark (typically 70% to 75% of OMV) that reflects an urgent 60-to-90 day distress liquidation. Commercial banks use FSV to set loan lending limits to ensure they are fully secured if default occurs.

Can diaspora investors conduct remote land verification safely?

Yes, over 40% of our verification briefs come from Nigerians in the UK, United States, Canada, UAE, and Europe. We provide a full remote due diligence package: on-site GPS beacon photos/video walk-throughs, certified AGIS title search reports, and official digital valuation seals delivered directly to your email, eliminating the need to send money blindly to relatives or unregistered middlemen.

What is a Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) and why does it matter?

A Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) is a structured legal and socio-economic framework used when acquiring land where local villages, crops, or communities reside. Executed under World Bank ESS5 and IFC PS5 standards, it ensures Project-Affected Persons (PAPs) are compensated at non-depreciated Full Replacement Value (FRV), preventing violent land clashes, court injunctions, and project stoppages.
